# Point of Sales and Inventory Management System

A web-based Point of Sales (POS) and Inventory Management System developed to automate sales transactions, inventory handling, and customer management for businesses.

## Project Overview

Efficient sales and inventory management is essential for smooth business operations. Manual systems often lead to errors in stock tracking, sales calculations, and customer handling.

This project provides a complete digital solution that allows businesses to:

- Manage products and stock
- Process sales transactions
- Generate invoices/receipts
- Handle customers
- Apply discounts
- Generate reports
- Receive low-stock alerts

The system improves accuracy, reduces manual work, and enhances overall business efficiency.
